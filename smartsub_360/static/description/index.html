SmartSub 360

Intelligent Subscription Management & Recurring Billing for Odoo 19 Community

Overview

SmartSub 360 brings enterprise-grade recurring billing and subscription contract lifecycle management to Odoo 19 Community Edition without any proprietary enterprise lock-in.

Key Capabilities

  • Flexible Billing Plans: Support for Daily, Weekly, Monthly, and Annual cadences with configurable grace periods.
  • Contract Lifecycle Engine: Manage agreements through Draft, In Progress, Paused, and Closed states with audit chatter.
  • Odoo Accounting Integration: Deep linkage with account.move for seamless invoice generation and payment tracking.
  • Multi-Company Security: Granular multi-company isolation and role-based permissions (User vs Administrator).
